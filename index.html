Web3 payments

Pay the way that works for you

Cash and international card payments are supported today. FreClean is developing Celo stablecoin payments through our infrastructure partner CeloHT, with the Valora wallet, so customers without a bank account can still pay digitally.

How Web3 payments work

FreClean does not issue its own token or blockchain. Web3 payments run on the existing Celo network through our partner CeloHT — a real infrastructure choice, not a marketing claim.
