Company description

Modern cleaning, products and opportunity.

FreClean is a cleaning-services and consumer-products business based in Léogâne, Haiti. Founded by Johnny Dubic on April 16, 2025, it offers professional cleaning enquiries and household-care and fragrance products, and is developing entrepreneurship and community-support work.
