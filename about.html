FreClean is a company delivering cleaning services, cleaning products, entrepreneurship support, and digital payment access, founded on April 16, 2025.

Founder

Johnny Dubic

Johnny Dubic founded FreClean in Léogâne, Ouest, Haiti, to build a professional cleaning company that also opens a path into entrepreneurship and digital payments for the people who work with it.

FreClean is actively seeking strategic investors and partners to expand its model across Haiti and future regional markets.

Governance

How FreClean is run

Founder-led

Johnny Dubic sets company direction and holds founder responsibilities defined in FreClean's governance policy.

Five-member management group

A five-member management group operates on a renewable five-year term cycle. Member identities are kept private and are not published.

Treasury controls

Corporate funds are not controlled by one person alone for significant transactions; multi-signature approval and spending thresholds apply as the treasury policy is finalized.

Governance structure, treasury policy, and management appointments are documented internally and shared once finalized. The site distinguishes verified facts from information still under review.
