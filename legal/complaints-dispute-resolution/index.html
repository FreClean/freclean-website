Complaints & Dispute Resolution

Clear process,
respectful resolution.

FreClean welcomes feedback on service quality, product issues, billing questions, communication, and any concern that may require a formal review.

Purpose

Prompt review.

We aim to resolve complaints fairly, promptly and in a way that is proportionate to the issue. This page sets out the public process for raising concerns about a product, service, payment, communication, or agreement.

Where possible, complaints should be raised directly with FreClean as soon as the issue is identified. Early communication helps us review the matter, gather relevant details, and identify a suitable remedy.

How to submit

Share the facts.

Customers may raise a complaint by email at freclean7@gmail.com or by phone at +1 (849) 388-1969. Please include the relevant date, service or product description, the issue experienced, any supporting photos or documents, and the expected resolution.

When a complaint relates to a specific order, quote, service request, or payment, FreClean may ask for the confirmation message, invoice, receipt, or other evidence needed to review the matter fairly.

Review process

Evidence and response.

FreClean reviews each complaint based on the information provided, the agreed scope of work or product details, and any record of communication between the parties. A complaint may lead to a clarification, correction, refund review, service remedy, apology, or other practical resolution.

Where a matter raises broader or more serious concerns, including product safety, misconduct, or repeated service failure, it may be escalated internally for a more formal review and a documented decision.

Resolution

Outcome and follow-up.

FreClean will communicate the outcome of the review as clearly as possible and explain whether the request is accepted, partially accepted, declined, or referred to a different process. If a dispute remains unresolved, a customer may request a reconsideration or may contact the relevant local authority or consumer protection channel, where applicable.

FreClean keeps records of complaints and resolution decisions to support accountability, consistency, and service improvement over time.

Contact

Questions or escalation.

For formal complaint or dispute concerns, email freclean7@gmail.com or call +1 (849) 388-1969. Read the Privacy Policy, Terms, and Legal Center for the broader framework governing public information and customer communications.
