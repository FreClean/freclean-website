Payments

FreClean supports three payment categories. Availability of each can depend on the service, location and order type.

Crypto

Pay with cUSD or CELO through the CeloHT dApp. FreClean verifies every transaction on-chain before confirming your order.

Card

Visa and Mastercard through a secure, certified payment processor. FreClean never stores your raw card details.

Cash

Available for eligible face-to-face transactions. Payment is confirmed once authorized FreClean staff record it.
