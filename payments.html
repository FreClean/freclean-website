Verified outcomes.

Payment method availability depends on the service, location and order type. Payment status is never confirmed by the frontend alone.

01 · Cash

Face-to-face

Cash transactions are recorded and reconciled by authorized FreClean staff where eligible.

02 · Card

International card

Card payments use a certified hosted processor flow. FreClean does not store raw card details.

03 · Crypto

CELO and USDm

Where enabled, the Core payment flow verifies chain, asset, recipient, amount and confirmations before changing payment status.

04 · Principle

Server-side truth

A frontend success message never makes a payment final. Verification belongs to the FreClean Core payment infrastructure.
