Digital payments on Celo, through CeloHT

FreClean is not a crypto company. It's a cleaning company that also accepts a stablecoin payment, verified the same way any other payment is verified.

Explained simply

Four questions, answered

What is Celo?

Celo is a public blockchain network built for fast, low-cost digital payments, including stablecoins whose value is tied to a currency like the US dollar.

What is a stablecoin?

A stablecoin is a digital asset designed to hold a steady value, unlike volatile cryptocurrencies, so a payment's worth doesn't change between checkout and confirmation.

How do I pay?

Select Web3 at FreClean checkout, scan the payment request with a compatible wallet, and confirm the amount.

How do I use Valora?

Valora is a mobile wallet built for Celo. Install it, add funds, and scan a FreClean payment request to pay.

Payment lifecycle

How a Web3 payment is verified

Every FreClean Web3 payment moves through the same tracked states — nothing is marked confirmed until it is verified on-chain.

01

Requested

Checkout generates a payment request.

02

Pending

Waiting for the customer's wallet to send funds.

03

Detected

A matching transaction is seen on Celo.

04

Verified

The transaction is checked against the request.

05

Confirmed

Order or booking is confirmed and a receipt issued.

Payments can also end in Failed, Expired, or Refunded where applicable.
