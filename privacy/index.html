Privacy

Respect for your
information.

This notice describes the information FreClean may receive when you use this public website or send a service enquiry.

What may be collected

Only what helps us respond.

Depending on the form you use, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, service type, property or location details, preferred date and time, payment preference and notes about your request.

FreClean uses this information to respond to enquiries, discuss service scope and availability, coordinate next steps and communicate about a request. A service request is not a confirmed booking until FreClean confirms it directly.

Sharing and retention

Handled for the request.

The static website does not collect card numbers, wallet credentials or private keys. If a configured public request endpoint is enabled, the form sends the submitted details to that endpoint over HTTPS. Otherwise, the website provides a verified email fallback and does not claim that an online submission was sent.

FreClean keeps enquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide requested services, meet operational needs or comply with applicable obligations. Payment providers and the CeloHT dApp are separate services with their own policies when a customer chooses those routes.
