Who We Are

A Haitian business with a practical purpose.

FreClean was founded in Haiti on April 16, 2025, and is rooted in Léogâne, Ouest. It brings professional cleaning services and consumer products together with an entrepreneurship initiative and community-oriented support.

FreClean's starting point is practical care for the spaces where people live, work, and welcome others. The business is developing related products, commercial relationships, and participation opportunities from that foundation, without overstating its current scale or reach.

Community Support

Support Entrepreneurship. Create Better Opportunities.

FreClean's entrepreneurship initiative includes a community-support and contribution component. Contributions may support eligible entrepreneurship initiatives, practical community-support activities, economic opportunity, and development of the entrepreneurship ecosystem.

These activities are related to FreClean's work, but they are not the same kind of transaction.

Customer paymentA customer's payment for a confirmed product or service arrangement.
Business revenueCommercial receipts from FreClean services or product sales; not a community contribution.
Affiliate earningsPotential commercial participation under applicable program terms.
Partnership fundingAny funding arrangement must be separately discussed and agreed; none is implied here.
Community contributionSupport for eligible initiatives; no financial return is promised.
InvestmentA distinct financial activity; community contributions are not presented as investments.

Contributors are not investors by virtue of making a contribution. Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible. FreClean does not claim nonprofit or charity status on this page.

How contributions work

Clear handling. No automatic claims.

  • Contributions are handled manually. The website does not automatically verify every transfer or issue a contribution receipt.
  • The founder currently manages contributed funds. FreClean intends to communicate how contributions are used and provide verified progress updates where applicable.
  • Keep the transaction reference and contact FreClean if you need to follow up. A submitted transaction reference is not itself confirmation of receipt.
  • No audited contribution report is published on this page. Stronger reporting and measurement are goals for future development, not claims about a system already in place.

For current contribution instructions, use FreClean's official entrepreneurship and payments pages and confirm details with the team before sending.

Privacy & dignity

Support should never require a person to give up their dignity or privacy.

Contributor privacy should be respected. Supported individuals and families should not be publicly identified without appropriate, informed consent.

Names, photographs, personal stories, and other identifying details should only be shared with consent. Sensitive personal information must not be exposed. A person can receive support without becoming a public story.

Our story

Started with care for everyday spaces.

Founded April 16, 2025 · Léogâne, Haiti

FreClean was founded in Haiti around cleaning services and products. From that starting point, the concept has grown to include household and fragrance products, professional cleaning enquiries, entrepreneurship, community support, and partnerships.

The idea is straightforward: cleaner environments can improve everyday life; useful products can create commercial opportunities; responsible business participation can support entrepreneurship; and entrepreneurship can contribute to stronger communities.

These are the principles guiding FreClean's development. They are not claims of measured outcomes or of activity beyond the company's current capacity.
