Privacy Policy

FreClean collects the information needed to provide a booking, order, or payment: name, contact details, service address, and payment confirmation data. Web3 payments store the network, asset, amount, and transaction reference — never wallet seed phrases or private keys. Data is not sold to third parties. Full policy: in development.

Terms of Service

Use of the FreClean website, booking system, and Web3 payment tools is subject to these terms. Full legal terms: in development.

Booking Policy

Bookings are confirmed once a cleaning team is assigned. Customers receive confirmation by phone or email before the scheduled date.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation windows and any applicable fees: in development.

Refund Policy

Refunds for cash and card payments follow standard processing timelines. Web3 refunds are issued on the same network as the original payment and are tracked with the same Requested → Confirmed status used for payments. Full policy: in development.

Payment Policy

FreClean accepts cash, international card payments, and supported Celo stablecoin payments through CeloHT. No payment is marked confirmed until it is verified. Full policy: in development.
