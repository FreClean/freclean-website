Build your own business on FreClean products

The FreClean Entrepreneur Program gives people training, product access, and a starter package to sell and distribute FreClean cleaning and fragrance products.

Program status: Launch planning

How the program is designed to work

01

Training

Orientation and business education before launch.

02

Product access

Access to the FreClean product catalog at entrepreneur pricing.

03

Starter package

A defined starter package of products and materials to begin selling.

04

Launch & sell

Sales, marketing, and customer management support.

05

Grow

Digital payments, distribution, and performance tracking as the business grows.

Eligibility

Open to individuals in FreClean's operating area who complete program orientation. Final eligibility criteria are being defined.

Pricing guidance

Wholesale and resale pricing guidance is provided once the product catalog and starter packages are finalized.

Digital payments

Entrepreneurs will be able to accept cash, card, and Web3 payments through the same FreClean payment system used company-wide.

FreClean does not promise income or guaranteed profits through the Entrepreneur Program. Earnings depend on individual sales performance.
